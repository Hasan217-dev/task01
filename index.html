Our Story

Started in a tiny kitchen, still made the same slow way

Sift & Sprinkle began as a weekend hobby — trays of cookies shared with neighbors. Twelve years later, we still measure flour by hand, whip our own buttercream, and never freeze a finished cake.

Every order is baked the same day it's picked up, because a cake should taste like it was made for you — not for a shelf.

🌾 Scratch-made daily 🚫 No preservatives 🎂 Custom orders welcome
